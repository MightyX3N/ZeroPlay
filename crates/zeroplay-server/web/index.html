Picture source

A capture card between the Xbox and the TV gives a cleaner picture than remote play. Remote play stays connected for the controllers.

ZeroPlay's container needs access to capture cards. Plug in a capture card that works like a webcam, and add these lines under zeroplay: in its compose file (they are in the repo's compose file), then recreate the container:

    volumes:
      - /dev:/dev
    device_cgroup_rules:
      - 'c 81:* rmw'
      - 'c 116:* rmw'
      - 'c 226:* rmw'

Also turn off HDCP on the Xbox (Settings → General → TV & display options → Video fidelity), or the card only gets a black picture.
